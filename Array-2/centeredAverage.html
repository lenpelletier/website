centeredAverage: average without the extremes

Return the "centered" average of an array of ints: the mean of the values, ignoring the largest and smallest. If there are several copies of the smallest (or largest) value, ignore just one copy. Use int division. The array has length 3 or more. Step through centeredAverage: one loop finds min, max and the total sum at the same time; then those two are taken back out.
